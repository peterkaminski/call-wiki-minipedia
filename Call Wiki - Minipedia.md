# Call Wiki - Minipedia

This folder documents the history, evolution, and current state of Pete Kaminski's "Call Wiki" / "Minipedia" invention — a method for transforming conversation recordings into richly interlinked wiki knowledge bases using AI.

See [[README]] for the full project overview, [[History and Evolution]] for the chronological story, and the [[Prompt Library/Prompt Library|Prompt Library]] for the actual prompt files with their lineage narrative — ready to copy and use.
